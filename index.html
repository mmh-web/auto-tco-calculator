Methodology

Depreciation: Year-0 basis = your purchase price. Years 1+ = MSRP × cumulative residual factors (market-anchored). Buying below MSRP reduces year-1 depreciation; subsequent market values follow the MSRP curve regardless of what you paid.

Financing: Full amortization — only interest counted as cost (principal is equity). Lease cost = all monthly payments + CCR + acquisition + disposition + excess mileage. No equity at lease end.

Opportunity cost: Compound annual return on down payment cash foregone, accumulated over ownership period. Toggle on/off above.

Maintenance & repairs: Age-based averages (Consumer Reports / RepairPal). $400–$2,400/yr maintenance scaling with age. $0 repairs yrs 1–3 (warranty), rising to ~$1,800/yr at yr 10. Adjust with multipliers.

Categories match: Edmunds True Cost to Own (depreciation, financing, insurance, taxes/fees, fuel, maintenance, repairs, EV credits) + KBB 5-Year Cost to Own + opportunity cost.
