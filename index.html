How costs are calculated:
• Depreciation: Market value follows MSRP × annual residual curve. Your basis = purchase price. Buying below MSRP = lower year-1 depreciation. Loaner/demo cars: enter starting odometer to reflect partial-year depreciation already incurred.
• Financing: Standard amortization; only interest is counted as cost (principal is equity). Lease: monthly payment × term + cap cost reduction + acquisition + disposition + excess mileage charges.
• Opportunity cost: Compound annual return on down payment / CCR cash foregone over ownership period.
• Taxes & fees: Sales tax on purchase price (year 1 only) + annual registration. EV/PHEV credits deducted in year 1.
• Insurance: Your base premium declining each year as vehicle value drops.
• Maintenance: Age-based averages from Consumer Reports / RepairPal, scaled by your multiplier.
• Repairs: $0 under warranty (yrs 1–3), rising sharply after year 5. Based on J.D. Power IQS data.
• Categories match: Edmunds TCO (depreciation, financing, insurance, taxes/fees, fuel, maintenance, repairs, EV credits) + KBB 5-Year Cost to Own + opportunity cost (yours to toggle).
